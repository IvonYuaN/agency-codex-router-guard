# Nuwa Distillation Principles

Distilled from `alchaincyf/nuwa-skill`.

## What Nuwa adds

Nuwa does not focus on imitation. It focuses on extracting a cognitive operating system.

Key layers to preserve:

- expression DNA
- mental models
- decision heuristics
- anti-patterns / value floor
- honest limits

## What Router Guard should borrow

- Do not reduce an agent to tone alone
- Prefer extracting how a specialist thinks over how they sound
- State honest limits explicitly
- Preserve anti-patterns, not just strengths

## Router Guard implications

When defining a routed squad or preset, capture:

- what mental model leads
- what decision heuristics matter
- what that agent refuses to optimize for
- what the limits of that lens are
